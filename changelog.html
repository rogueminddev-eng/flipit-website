FlipIt updates

Download FlipIt 1.2.0 for Windows and Android from the homepage.

1.2.0 · 30 October 2026

  • PDF tools on Windows and Android: merge, split, extract, remove and rotate pages; compress PDFs; save pages as pictures; add page numbers and watermarks.
  • Make scanned PDFs searchable so recognised English text can be searched and copied.
  • Draw a signature or use a picture of it on a PDF. This is a visual mark, not a certified digital signature.
  • Android: scan several pages into one PDF, adjusting the four corners of each page.
  • Picture size options show measured output sizes. Scanning and size measurement have performance improvements.
  • Android: corrected mono audio encoding.
  • English text recognition can make mistakes; check important results. Compression savings depend on the PDF.
  • The Windows installer remains unsigned and may trigger SmartScreen.

1.1.0 · 27 October 2026

  • First public Android and Windows downloads, with checksums and library notices.
  • Local document, image and audio conversion; password protect and unlock for supported documents.
  • Batch conversion on Windows. Single-file conversion and camera scanning with four-corner crop on Android.
  • Android private-file cleanup fixes. English OCR results should be checked against the original.
  • The Windows installer is unsigned and can trigger SmartScreen.

27 October 2026 · Website and privacy update

  • Clarified the current apps: batch conversion on Windows, and camera scanning with four-corner crop on Android.
  • Explained private scan retention, temporary-file cleanup, sharing and website storage in the privacy page.
  • Clarified that website visits and download totals are approximate counts, not unique people or installations.
