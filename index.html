Offline file converter · Windows and Android

Convert files without sending them anywhere.

FlipIt turns PDFs into spreadsheets, Word documents into PDFs, and photos and audio into the formats you need — on your own PC, or on your own phone. Hidden metadata comes off on the way out, and nothing is uploaded, because there's nowhere to upload it to.

The app

One window. Drop, choose, convert.

Drop
Drag files onto FlipIt or click Browse. Mix in files of another type and FlipIt says how many it skipped.
Choose

Convert

On the way out

What happens to a file after it's converted

Metadata

Hidden details come off

Photos lose their camera and GPS tags. Word and Excel files lose their author, title and last-modified-by. PDFs lose their document info. It's on by default — switch it off for any conversion where you want to keep them.

EXIF · dc:creator · cp:lastModifiedBy · /Info

Encryption

Lock it with a password

Drop a PDF, Word or Excel file and FlipIt shows whether it's protected. Protect saves a password-protected copy in the same format and leaves your original untouched; Unlock saves an unprotected copy. You can also lock a file as you convert it. Acrobat, Edge, Word and Excel ask for the password before showing anything.

AES-256 · .pdf · .docx · .xlsx · originals untouched

Control

Stop whenever you like

Press Stop mid-conversion and FlipIt finishes the step it's on, then stops. Files already converted are kept, and an unfinished file never lands next to your originals.

Partial output is never saved

Formats

Sixteen conversions, one window

Drag in a single file or a batch of the same type. Converted files are saved next to the originals unless you pick another folder, and PDFs report progress page by page with a time estimate.

Documents

  • .docx→.pdf
  • .pdf→.docx

Tables

  • .pdf→.xlsx
  • .pdf→.csv
  • .csv→.xlsx
  • .xlsx→.csv
  • .xlsx→.pdf

A table that spans many pages lands on one sheet. Scanned tables are read with OCR and keep their columns. A workbook saved as PDF keeps every sheet, each starting a new page under its name.

Images

  • .jpg→.png
  • .png→.jpg

Any picture can be made smaller too: large, medium or small, or just left as it is. Camera and location tags are removed from every output, and a photograph turned to Word comes out as the text read from it. The Android app can also scan a page with the camera.

Questions

Common questions

Why does Windows say "Windows protected your PC"?

Windows SmartScreen shows that warning for apps from publishers it doesn't know yet, and FlipIt doesn't have a code-signing certificate so far. Check that the installer's SHA-256 matches the one on this page, then choose More info and Run anyway.

Does FlipIt need an internet connection?

No. Once it's installed, converting, removing metadata and locking files all happen on your PC. There's no account, no sign-in and no background update check.

Which files can I lock with a password?

PDF, Word (.docx) and Excel (.xlsx) files, either as they are with Protect or while converting to one of those formats. They have password protection built in, so a protected file opens with a password prompt in Acrobat, Edge, Word or Excel. Text and CSV files can be protected by converting them to PDF or Excel. Images and audio have no password feature.

I've forgotten the password for a locked file.

There's no way back in — FlipIt never stores or sends passwords, which is also why it asks you to type the password twice. If you still have the original file, protect it again with a new password.

What happens to my original files?

Nothing. FlipIt only reads them and writes the converted file next to them, or into the folder you choose. If a file with the same name is already there, the new one is saved as "name (1)".

Can FlipIt read scanned PDFs?

For PDF to Excel or CSV, yes: pages without real text are read with OCR in English, and each line becomes a row. Clean printed scans work best; photos of pages and handwriting don't come out well. PDF to Word needs a PDF with real text.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Settings, go to Apps › Installed apps, find FlipIt and choose Uninstall.
